Lexoria Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Overview

Lexoria is a mobile word roguelite game developed by Gardiner Interactive. This policy explains what information may be collected, used, or shared when you use Lexoria.

Information Lexoria collects directly

Lexoria does not require an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, messages, photos, payment information, or other personally submitted information through the game.

Local gameplay data

Lexoria may store gameplay progress, run state, settings, statistics, achievements, unlocks, and similar game data locally on your device. This data is used to make the game function, restore runs, and remember your preferences.

Advertising and third-party services

Lexoria uses Google AdMob to show ads, including rewarded ads and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect or process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app activity, diagnostics, and other data as described by Google, depending on your device settings, region, consent choices, and Google services configuration.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services at Google Partner Sites and review Google's Privacy Policy at Google Privacy Policy.

Consent choices

In regions where consent is required, advertising consent choices may be requested through Google's consent tools or your device settings. You can also limit ad personalization from your Android advertising privacy settings where available.

Children's privacy

Lexoria is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob may be handled according to those providers' policies and used for advertising, fraud prevention, service operation, analytics, diagnostics, and compliance purposes.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the services we control. No mobile app or internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Lexoria changes. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact Gardiner Interactive at gardiner.brandonc@gmail.com.
